Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

Sunny Apps Ltd ("Sunny", "we", "us") operates the sunnypubs.app website and the Sunny web application at visit.sunnypubs.app. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data.

2. What data we collect

  • Location data: When you grant permission, we use your device's location to show nearby venues. We do not store your precise location on our servers.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymous analytics (pages visited, features used) to improve the service.
  • Account data: If you create an account, we store your email address and saved venues.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies for site functionality and optional analytics cookies with your consent.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the Sunny service
  • To show you nearby venues with current sun conditions
  • To save your preferences and favourite venues
  • To send service-related communications (if you opt in)

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate Sunny (hosting, analytics)
  • Law enforcement if required by law

5. Data retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. You can also object to or restrict processing. Contact us at hello@sunnypubs.app to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@sunnypubs.app.
