You know the moment. The sun drops low, everything turns warm and gold, and the drink in your hand suddenly looks a lot better. That's golden hour, and it might be the best time of day to be sitting outside at the pub.

## What is golden hour?

Golden hour is the short stretch of time just before sunset, when the sun is low in the sky. There's no official definition, but it's usually counted as the time when the sun is within about 6 degrees of the horizon, according to [timeanddate.com](https://www.timeanddate.com/astronomy/golden-hour.html).

## Why the light looks so good

When the sun is low, its light travels through much more of the atmosphere to reach you. Along the way, a lot of the blue light gets scattered, so what's left looks warmer, more orange and softer.

The low angle also means:

- **Gentler light.** It's less harsh than the midday sun, so it's easier on the eyes.
- **Long shadows and glowing edges.** That's why photos taken at this time look so good.
- **Milder warmth.** You still feel the sun, but without the midday heat.

## When is it?

Despite the name, golden hour is often shorter than an hour. It's shorter near the equator and longer the further you are from it.

As a rough guide, start looking about an hour before sunset. In early November, for example:

- **Sydney:** sunset is around 7.25pm, with golden hour from about 6.50pm.
- **London:** sunset is around 4.30pm, with golden hour from about 3.40pm.

Check your local sunset time on the day, as it changes through the year.

## How to catch it at the pub

- **Face west.** The evening sun sets in the west, so look for a beer garden or terrace with a clear view in that direction. Our guide on [which beer gardens get the most sun](/blog/which-beer-gardens-get-the-most-sun/) explains more.
- **Watch for buildings and trees.** When the sun is this low, even a small building can block it. A rooftop often keeps the sun for longer, as we explain in [rooftop bar or beer garden](/blog/rooftop-bar-or-beer-garden/).
- **Get there early.** The best seats in the sun go fast, so arrive before golden hour starts.
- **Bring a layer.** It cools down quickly once the sun goes.

## Summing up

Golden hour is the warm, soft light just before sunset, and it's a lovely time for a drink outside. Check when the sun sets, find a spot facing west and get there a little early.
