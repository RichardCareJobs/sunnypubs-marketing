If you love a drink in the sun, where you live makes a big difference. Some cities get more than twice as much sunshine as others. So which places are best for a drink outside?

## How we compared

We looked at the average number of sunshine hours each city gets in a year. The figures below are rounded long-term averages. They vary a little depending on the weather station and the years measured, so treat them as a guide.

For comparison, London gets around 1,500 hours of sunshine a year.

## The sunniest of all

**Yuma, Arizona** holds the [Guinness World Record](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/66545-most-sunshine) for the most sunshine, with the sun out for about 90% of daylight hours. That's around 4,000 hours a year.

It's in the desert, though, so summer is far too hot for sitting outside in the afternoon. Winter and spring are the times to go.

## Sunny and pleasant

The best cities for an outdoor drink are sunny without being scorching. Most of these have a Mediterranean climate, with warm, dry summers and mild winters:

- **Los Angeles:** around 3,250 hours. Sunny all year, with mild winters.
- **Perth:** around 3,200 hours, the most of any Australian capital. That's an average of 8.8 hours of sun a day, according to the [Bureau of Meteorology](https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/current/annual/wa/archive/2008.perth.shtml).
- **Cape Town:** around 3,000 hours. Summer, from December to March, is dry and sunny.
- **Valletta, Malta:** around 3,000 hours, one of the sunniest capitals in Europe.
- **Seville:** around 2,900 hours. Spring and autumn are best, as summer often goes above 35°C.
- **Athens:** around 2,800 hours, with plenty of rooftop bars looking up at the Acropolis.

## Hot and very sunny

**Dubai** gets around 3,600 hours of sunshine a year, according to [NOAA climate averages](https://ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/normals/WMO/1981-2010/RA-II/UnitedArabEmirates/graph/41194_united_arab_emirates_dubai_international_airport.pdf). Summer is extremely hot, so the outdoor season runs from about November to March. Drinks are served in licensed venues, such as hotel bars.
