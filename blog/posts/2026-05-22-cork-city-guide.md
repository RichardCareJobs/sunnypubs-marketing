Cork is small enough to walk and varied enough to keep surprising you, which makes it the perfect city to plan around the sun rather than around a fixed list of stops.

## Let the light set the route

Begin near the English Market while the lanes are still bright, then drift toward the river as the buildings start to throw shade. The quays open up to the sky, and the south-facing stretches hold sun right through the early evening.

- Lunch tables that catch the midday sun near the market.
- A riverside stretch that stays lit as the lanes go cool.
- A western terrace for the final hour of gold.

:::tip
Build your afternoon backwards from where the sun will be at six, and walk toward it. Sunny shows you exactly where that is.
:::
