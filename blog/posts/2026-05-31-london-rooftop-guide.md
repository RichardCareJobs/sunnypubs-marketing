London is a city that eats its own sunlight. Tall buildings, narrow streets, and a low evening sun mean ground-level gardens go into shade earlier than you'd think. The answer is simple: go up.

## Height buys you an extra hour

A rooftop two or three storeys up clears most of the terraces around it, which on a clear evening is worth a solid extra hour of gold. We've leaned on this trick all over town, from the warehouses of Peckham to the glass of the City.

- Peckham — a multi-storey car-park bar that's pure west-facing sun till close.
- Shoreditch — a hotel terrace that catches light from three sides.
- Southbank — wide open to the river and the low evening sun.

The map does the heavy lifting here. Filter to rooftops, check the live count, and let the height take care of the rest.

:::tip
