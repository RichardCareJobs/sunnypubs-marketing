When we started, Sunny was a Dublin obsession. Then Cork, then London. Today we're flipping the switch on two more: Birmingham and Manchester are live, with full sun signals on every venue that has a chair under the sky.

## What 'live' means

Every pub, beer garden and rooftop in the new cities now shows real-time sun, the same way Dublin always has — full sun, partial or shade, mapped to the actual tables. No staged listings, no pay-to-rank, just where the light is.

> If your city isn't on Sunny yet, hang tight. We're adding new ones constantly — and the list only grows.
