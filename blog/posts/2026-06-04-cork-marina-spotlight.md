Every city has one spot that quietly out-suns everywhere else, and in Cork the regulars have known about this one for years. We're letting the secret out gently.

## Why it works

The deck faces almost due south across open water, which means nothing — no terrace, no tree, no tall neighbour — gets between you and the sky from late morning onward. Full sun reads on the Sunny badge from about eleven, and it holds.

- South-facing across the water — full sun most of the day.
- Wide tables, room to spread out, dog-friendly down the quiet end.
- Light breeze off the Lee keeps it comfortable when the sun is strongest.

It's not fancy and it doesn't need to be. This is a place that understands the assignment: a flat surface for a glass, an unobstructed sky, and somewhere to be when the forecast finally turns your way.
