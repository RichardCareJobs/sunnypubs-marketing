People love to point out that Birmingham has more miles of canal than Venice. Fewer point out the lovely side effect: all that flat water throws light back up onto the terraces beside it.

## Borrowed light

This Brindleyplace garden faces the water, so even as the sun drops it keeps catching reflected light off the canal — a soft, warm second act after the direct sun has gone. The Sunny badge holds at 'some sun' well past the point you'd expect.

- Canalside and west-facing — direct sun late, reflected sun later still.
- Big shared tables, good for a group that grows as the evening goes.
- A short walk from New Street, so it's an easy after-work pivot.

:::tip
Water-facing terraces are a Sunny favourite — filter for canalside and you'll squeeze an extra half hour out of any evening.
:::
