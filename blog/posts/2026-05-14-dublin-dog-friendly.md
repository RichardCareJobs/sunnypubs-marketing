Some sunny spots are about the view. This one is about staying put — a walled Stoneybatter garden that catches the sun early, holds the heat in its old brick, and welcomes the dog like a regular.

## Warmth that lingers

The high south-facing wall is the secret. It soaks up sun all afternoon and radiates it back, so the garden stays warm even as the light softens — the Sunny badge sits on full sun for hours.

- Walled and south-facing — sheltered, warm and bright.
- Water bowls and shade corners for the dogs.
- Long benches that fill up slowly into the evening.
