We built Sunny to answer one question: where's the sun right now? But you told us, loudly and often, that 'right now' wasn't quite enough. You wanted to know whether it would still be sunny by the time you'd walked there. So we built that.

## What's new

Tap any venue and you'll see a two-hour forecast strip — full sun, partial, or shade, mapped to the exact tables. It uses the same live sun model that powers the map, just projected forward, so you can leave the house knowing the light will hold.

- Two-hour sun forecast on every venue card.
- A 'still sunny when you arrive' badge for places within walking distance.
- Faster map loading and sharper pins on the live count.

> The sun's already moving. Now you can move with it instead of chasing it.

It's live today for everyone, everywhere Sunny works — no update needed, no sign-up, still free. Open the map and give it a go.
