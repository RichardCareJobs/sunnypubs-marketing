Let's get the joke out of the way: yes, it rains in Manchester. But the city has a habit of clearing into a gorgeous golden evening, and when it does, the Northern Quarter is ready for it.

## Yards, roofs and the gaps between

The grid of old warehouses creates pockets — interior yards that trap warmth, and rooftops that suddenly clear the brick once you're up a flight of stairs. The difference between a sunny table and a shaded one here is often a single doorway.

- A converted-warehouse roof that's west-facing and wide open.
- A cobbled yard that holds heat long after the sun's gone behind the buildings.
- A first-floor terrace that catches the gap between two old mills.

This is exactly the kind of place a live map earns its keep — the sun moves fast between buildings, and the count tells you where it's landed right now.

:::tip
When Manchester clears, it clears fast. Have Sunny open and ready so you don't miss the window.
:::
