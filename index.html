How it works

Three taps to get you in the sun.

We built Sunny to cut out the noise. No fiddly filters or messy menus. Just find a spot, get the directions or an Uber and you're there before you know it.

01

Open the map

Browse every pub and beer garden with outdoor seating near you, alongside the local weather forecast for the afternoon.

02

Pick a sunny spot

Tap any pin to see the sun forecast for the next two hours, plus photos, hours and how busy it is.

03

Go and soak up the rays

Walk, drive, public transport or take an Uber. You'll be drenched in vitamin D in no time.
